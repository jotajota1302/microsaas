Fair questions

What if the story does not convince me?

You read it before paying. You can ask for two changes («more of the grandmother», «less fear on page 6») and we rewrite it. If it still is not right, you do not pay.

How long does it take?

The written story, under a minute. The two sample pages, another minute. The complete book reaches your inbox, reviewed, the same day.

Will it look like my child?

It will look like how you describe them: hair, glasses, skin tone. We ask for no photos, so it is not a portrait — it is a character of their own, consistent throughout the book.

Do you send a printed copy?

Not yet: for now it is a PDF, ready to read on screen or print at home. If you would want it on paper, tell us from the story and we will let you know.
