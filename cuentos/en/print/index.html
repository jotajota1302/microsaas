From PDF to paper

How to get your story on paper

The story is yours and the PDF is ready to print. There are three ways to have it bound, and none of them goes through us: pick by what you want to spend and when you need it.

Print it as many times as you like for your own family: at home, at a copy shop or at a printer. It is your book.

What you need to know about the file

Size
18 square pages, 20 × 20 cm (about 8 × 8 in)
Colour
Full colour, plus 4 black-and-white colouring pages
Margins
Safe margins included: no need to trim to the millimetre
What it is made for
Screens and home printing. It looks great on A4 or Letter; on a professional press at a large size the illustrations read a little softer

The three ways

Today · at home

On your own printer

  • Print double-sided and choose “fit to page”: the square lands centred on A4 or Letter.
  • Use 120-160 gsm paper if you can. It changes how the book feels and stops the ink showing through.
  • Print the four colouring pages loose, so they can be painted flat on the table.
  • A long-arm stapler along the spine, or a folder with sleeves, and it is a book.

Today · around the corner

At a copy shop

  • Take it on your phone or a USB stick, or email it over. Nothing to prepare.
  • Ask for: colour, double-sided, 160 gsm paper, spiral or thermal binding.
  • It is 18 colour pages, so ask the price first. As a rough guide it tends to land around €10-15, depending on the town and the paper.
  • This is the best result for the money.

In a few days · online

At a printer that takes a PDF

  • Some printers make a single copy from your PDF, cover and all: Blurb (“upload your PDF”) and Lulu (it asks for the inside pages and the cover separately).
  • Pick the square format closest to 20 × 20 cm; their site tells you if something does not fit.
  • Some require an even page count. Ours is 18, which is even, so it goes through as it is.
  • We have no deal with them and earn nothing if you use them: they are here because they accept a PDF and a single copy.

What about photo book services?

Services like Shutterfly, Photobox or Hofmann are built around their own editor and do not accept a finished PDF. You can still get there by dropping each page in as a “full-page photo”, but it is a chunk of work. If you want to go that way, write to us from your story page and we will send you the pages as separate images, so all you have to do is drag them in.
