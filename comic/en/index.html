What people ask

Can they be in their favourite series?

No, and that is on purpose. Using someone else’s characters is illegal and, more to the point, it makes a worse gift: an imitation always loses to the original. What you can choose is the kind of story — powers academy, creature hunters, mechs, mystery — and the art style. It feels like what they love, and it is theirs.

Do you need a photo?

We never ask for photos, of them or anyone else. You describe them from a few lists. Safer, and it works better.

Is this made with AI?

Yes, and we say so inside the comic. The artwork and the first draft of the script are generated; the structure, the lettering and the review are ours. No script ships without an editor — and a person — having read it.

What if they do not like it?

That is what the free preview is for: you read the whole story before paying. If it does not land, you do not pay.

Can it be printed?

The PDF is made to read on screen and to print at home. If enough people want a proper printed edition we will make one — tell us when you order.
